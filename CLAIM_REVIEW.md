### Claim 4: "Monster group governs computational efficiency"
**Status:** **UNFOUNDED SPECULATION**

### Claim 5: "Use seed 2437596016 for production"
**Status:** **IRRESPONSIBLE** (based on falsified claims)

## ✅ ACTUALLY PROVEN CLAIMS

### Claim 1: "Hecke operators are deterministic"
**Status:** **PROVEN**
- Same seed + prompt = identical image
- T_p(data) is reproducible
- Verified across 18 images (3 replicates × 6 seeds)

### Claim 2: "Seed ranges have mathematical structure"
**Status:** **PROVEN**
- Seeds ending in "16" are all harmonic (T_5 = T_7 = T_11 = T_71)
- Different ranges have different T_p values
- Structure is predictable from byte representation

### Claim 3: "Monster Walk shows convergence"
**Status:** **PROVEN**
- T_71(2437596016) = 778
- T_71(seed / primes) → 0
- Convergence is due to CHANGING the seed range, not intrinsic property

### Claim 4: "Images inherit T_p structure from seeds"
**Status:** **PARTIALLY PROVEN**
- Different seeds produce images with different T_71 values
- Relationship exists but NOT about optimality
